# Projects

rejekt.ai - Generative AI Interview Preparation Platform (Jan 2026 - Present), live at https://www.rejekt.ai
- Live Generative AI platform with 6 interview modes generating personalized technical, behavioral, and company-specific mock interviews with OpenAI and Google Gemini.
- LangGraph adaptive engine keeps multi-turn context, asks follow-up questions, and scores answers on technical accuracy, STAR structure, communication, filler words, and missing concepts.
- End-to-end RAG with SentenceTransformers embeddings and ChromaDB, grounded in the resume and job description; tool/data integrations via Model Context Protocol (MCP).
- Real-time voice interviews with Whisper speech-to-text and text-to-speech.
- Python/FastAPI services on PostgreSQL and Redis, deployed on AWS ECS Fargate, RDS, S3, ALB, CloudWatch with Docker, GitHub Actions, and pytest.
- Ingestion pipelines across 19 job sources: 2.02M+ listings processed, about 115,600 qualified records kept.

Beyond Sight - Audible Graphs for the Visually Impaired (Sep 2023 - Apr 2024), University of North Texas
- ML accessibility system converting bar, line, and pie charts into audio narratives and structured text.
- Fine-tuned an ImageNet-pretrained ResNet (PyTorch, TensorFlow): 92% accuracy in chart classification and data extraction.
- OCR + NLP pipeline (Pandas, NumPy, spaCy, Tesseract) extracting titles, axes, legends, and values at 85% precision.
- Co-authored the paper in ACM W4A '24; Accessibility Challenge Judges' Award.

Resume Chatbot (In Progress)

Accessible Weather and Shelter Finder

Dynamic Pricing Prediction for Cabs
